About Pocket Mechanic

A place to keep track of your vehicles and the parts you buy or plan to buy for each one.

It started as a fix for receipts, bookmarks and notes spread across too many apps. Register a vehicle once, then every part you log or find is filed under it.

  1. Vehicle records

    Year, make, model and an optional nickname for each vehicle, with a running count of logged parts.

  2. Parts by category

    Each part is filed as maintenance, an accessory or a modification, with an optional link to where you bought it.

  3. Parts search

    Searches Google for parts matching your exact year, make and model. When the search service is unavailable, it links straight to AutoZone, RockAuto, Amazon and Advance Auto Parts.
